Protect Yourself Against Rabies

Welcome to RabiesCare, an educational platform designed to provide information about rabies, recognize possible symptoms and understand how to prevent this dangerous disease.

What Is Rabies?

Learn about the disease and how it spreads.

Rabies is a viral disease that affects the central nervous system of mammals, including humans and animals.

The virus is usually transmitted through the saliva of an infected animal, commonly through bites and scratches or saliva entering broken skin or mucous membranes.

Rabies is preventable with appropriate vaccination and timely medical treatment after exposure.

Animals That Can Transmit Rabies

Learn which animals can carry and transmit the rabies virus.

🐕

Dogs

Infected dogs can transmit rabies through bites and saliva entering broken skin.

🐈

Cats

Cats can become infected and transmit the virus through bites or scratches contaminated with saliva.

🦇

Bats

Bats are important rabies carriers. Avoid touching or handling them.

🦊

Foxes

Foxes can carry rabies in some regions. Avoid contact with wild foxes.

🦝

Raccoons

Raccoons can transmit rabies in regions where the virus circulates.

🐾

Skunks and Other Mammals

Skunks and other infected mammals can also transmit the rabies virus.

Important Information

Rabies can affect all mammals. Not every animal carries the virus, and the species alone cannot determine whether an animal is infected.

Never touch an unfamiliar or potentially sick animal.

Common Rabies Symptoms

Symptoms can vary depending on the stage of the disease.

🌡️

Fever

Elevated body temperature and general discomfort.

🤕

Headache

Pain or discomfort in the head.

⚡

Tingling

Unusual sensations near the site of an exposure.

💧

Swallowing Problems

Difficulty swallowing or painful throat spasms.

🧠

Confusion

Changes in mental state or unusual behavior.

💦

Hydrophobia

Difficulty or spasms associated with attempts to drink water.

Warning: Symptoms alone cannot confirm rabies. Never wait for symptoms after possible exposure.

How to Prevent Rabies

Follow these important prevention recommendations.

🐶

Vaccinate Pets

Keep dogs and cats vaccinated against rabies according to veterinary recommendations.

🚫

Avoid Wild Animals

Never approach or touch unfamiliar wild or potentially sick animals.

🧼

Wash Wounds

Wash bites or scratches immediately with soap and running water for 15 minutes.

🏥

Seek Medical Help

Contact a healthcare professional immediately after possible exposure.

  • Do not touch animals behaving unusually.
  • Teach children to stay away from unfamiliar animals.
  • Report potentially rabid animals to local authorities.
  • Follow professional medical and veterinary advice.
